Leadership: executive counsel for charity leaders

Start a conversation

Leading a charity is harder than most people think

Charity leaders carry a cause, a team, a board, a budget that rarely stretches far enough, and supporters who each expect to be heard. Most of them do it for less than they'd earn elsewhere. Many of them do it without anyone in the building they can talk to honestly.

That's where we come in.

What we do

We give executive counsel to the people leading charities. Sometimes that means a regular conversation with a CEO who wants a sounding board. Sometimes it means sitting with a senior team while they work out what they're really trying to achieve, and what it'll take to get there. Sometimes a founder needs help letting go of the bits they no longer need to hold.

We can help you with:

  • CEO and founder supportA confidential, experienced voice outside your organisation, on the decisions that keep you up at night.
  • Senior team strategyHelping your leadership team agree a clear vision, aims and objectives, and turn them into a plan people can follow.
  • Structure and growthWorking out how your team should be shaped, who you need next, and what you can stop doing.
  • Leadership recruitmentHelping boards find leaders who are genuinely inclined towards the things the organisation needs most.
  • Independent thoughtA fresh pair of eyes when you're too close to something to see it clearly.

Why leadership comes first

Charities often call us about income. More often than not, we end up talking about leadership. Fundraisers, comms teams and campaigners all do their best work when the people at the top know where they're going and can explain it simply. When leaders haven't agreed the vision, budgeted for it, or found a way to prove their impact, even the best fundraiser in the country will struggle.

So we start with the people leading. Get that right, and everything else has somewhere to stand.

Who we are

Dan Lane spent sixteen years at Christians Against Poverty, much of it as de facto chief of staff to the CEO and founder, before leading fundraising at Church Army. He has sat in the room where the hard decisions get made, and he's been the one who has to go and make them work afterwards. We bring that experience to every leader we work with: honest, practical, occasionally blunt, and always on your side.
