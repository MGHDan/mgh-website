However you need us

No two charities need quite the same thing, so we don't sell a fixed package. We work out with you what will actually help, and we're honest when something isn't the right fit.

Three ways we can help

  • StrategyWe help you and your team agree where you're going and plan how to get there.
  • ConsultancyWe give you advice, challenge and a second pair of eyes, for a few days or on an ongoing retainer.
  • Hands-on deliveryWe roll up our sleeves and do the work, from running donor events to leading your whole fundraising function.

Working with charities of every size

We work with large national charities and small local ones. We know smaller charities have tighter budgets, so we'll always look for a way to shape the work to what you can afford.
