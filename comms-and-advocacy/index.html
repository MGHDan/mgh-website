Getting your cause heard

You can be doing remarkable work and still be the best-kept secret in the country. Charities that grow are the ones people can find, understand and talk about. That means telling your story clearly, consistently, and to the people who can help.

What we do

  • Supporter communicationsStories, newsletters, appeals and updates that bring supporters closer to your work.
  • Messaging and positioningHelping you say what you do, and why it matters, in words people remember.
  • Advocacy and public affairsHelping you speak up to decision-makers on behalf of the people you serve.
  • Video and content strategyDeciding what to make, who it's for and how you'll know it worked.

Why it matters

We believe charities need to be findable before they can be fundable. Donors, partners and decision-makers all look you up before they say yes. When your communications are clear and your voice is confident, your fundraisers and your leaders both find doors opening more easily.
