Raising more, for longer

Most charities call us because they need more money, and usually they need it quickly. We understand that. But we've learned that the fastest route to more income is rarely the shortcut. Charities that raise well year after year are the ones that invest in fundraising early, give it time to work, and treat their supporters as people rather than wallets.

We help you do exactly that.

What we do

We can step in at whatever level you need, from a few days of advice to running your whole fundraising function.

  • Fundraising strategyA clear, realistic plan that ties your income to your vision, your budget and the impact you can prove.
  • Major donors and philanthropyFinding, meeting and building lasting relationships with the people who can give generously.
  • Trusts and foundationsApplications that funders want to say yes to, and a pipeline that keeps them coming.
  • Supporter givingRegular giving, appeals and campaigns that keep your supporters close and giving.
  • EventsDonor events and gatherings that leave people inspired.
  • Interim and fractional fundraisingWe run your fundraising for you while you build your team, or instead of hiring one.

What we believe

Fundraising is relational. Every charity rightly believes its cause matters most, but to a donor you're one of many. As we like to put it: you're one of their charities, they're not your donor. We help you earn your place.

We also believe fundraisers can't do it alone. When leaders set a clear vision, budget for it and show what difference it makes, fundraisers have something to work with. When they don't, even brilliant fundraisers struggle. That's why we often work with your leadership team as well as your fundraisers.
